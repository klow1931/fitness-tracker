# Coach Clarity & First-Week Athlete Pilot (v3.59)

Manual field acceptance checklist; **not yet passed**. Keep synthetic and real athlete records separated, use test copies for restore exercises, and obtain consent before collecting pilot feedback. Avoid promising physiological or medical conclusions.

## Day-one beginners

- Start without a profile or training history. Time from opening Home to a reviewed, unscheduled workout draft; count taps and inspect any errors.
- State training days and equipment in conversation, review the proposed preference card, cancel once and verify nothing changes, then explicitly save a revised profile.
- Ask for a strength program with incomplete load history. Coach must explain which exact maxes, exercise mapping and RPE evidence are missing, and still identify a safe next planning step.
- Check 320–430 px iPhone/Android screens, enlarged type, VoiceOver/TalkBack, touch and keyboard activation, dark/light themes and poor connectivity.

## Experienced lifters and Keith's mock-meet cycle

- Request an 8/12/16/20/24-week cycle, including “20-week meet prep,” without letting chat infer a meet date or a training max. Verify the requested total survives the 6-week lift-setup preview and maps to the independently reviewed meet timeline.
- In the mock-meet case, record true logged sets, load, reps and actual RPE throughout the cycle. Verify training max, current best, estimated capacity and kg/lb display remain distinct.
- Check Coach after sparse data, a real matched-rep load improvement and several RPE-aware exposures; it must not invent a physiological explanation or authorize unreviewed progression.
- At each week/phase review, record whether proposed keep/increase/reduce/insufficient decisions are understandable and whether the athlete accepts, modifies or ignores them.

## Device durability & shipping

- On actual Android and iPhone builds: save mid-workout, force-close, relaunch, log offline, fill device storage in a controlled test, upgrade from build 33402 to 33403, and verify no duplicate or lost actual sets.
- Export, wipe **test-device** app data only after verifying a valid export, restore and compare session identities/counts/units. Also validate recovery after phone loss/uninstall using an independently tested production backup before claiming sync durability.
- Record device/OS/build, exact action, expected/observed result, screenshot, and severity. Any data loss, incorrect kg/lb conversion, unsafe program adjustment or broken save blocks beta acceptance.
- Do not ship on synthetic checks alone. Native source builds, privacy/backend setup, signing, store declarations and multi-athlete follow-through are separate signoffs.
