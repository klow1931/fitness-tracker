# Loadnote v2.97.0 — Dedicated Olympic & Athlete Planners

Adult, reviewed plans now distinguish technical weightlifting, strength support, conditioning, speed, jump/landing contacts and agility. Select a matching structured goal, confirmed exercise/training-mode identities, actual equipment, explicit loads/distance/dose, season, practices/games and execution/stop protocols. Olympic lifting requires reported coaching support and a named reported coach review. Reported identities and technique quality are not independently verified.

Review all weeks before saving. Schedule separately without replacing Calendar sessions, logged training or overlapping reviewed cycles. Competition days and their preceding days are protected; nearby event dates, restrictions, discomfort and return-to-training context require an appropriate separate reviewed plan. Defaults, software bounds and task ordering do not establish an individually validated dose. Loads stay fixed; optional lower-volume weeks reduce rounds/minutes and effort caps, not load automatically.

Calendar opens a dedicated actual-session recorder. Technical made/missed/unknown outcomes enter the existing Olympic practice journal; speed/jump/agility observations enter a separate revisioned athletic journal. Neither enters strength PRs/estimated maxes. Only actually performed strength/conditioning enters those workout categories. A linked session log records that the session was reported, not that all targets were completed. Unknown effort, skipped work and unknown quality remain explicit. Coach and Decisions share read-only sport-plan evidence without inferring power, transfer, technical readiness or progression.

Schema 29 preserves `sportPrograms` and `athleticPractice` across backup, import, recovery and sync; older manifests retain their checksum basis. Version 2.97.0, unsigned build 29700. Native packaging/source checks are not compilation, signing or device acceptance.

Sport-session drafts recover after reload, failed saves retain observations, and explicit skipping never fabricates successful attempts. Extra already-performed sets/attempts can be reported without expanding reviewed targets. Incomplete dose/effort evidence remains visible; effort above a reviewed strength cap requests coach review. Practice targets are rejected by the ordinary strength logger. A declined service-worker registration no longer leaves broken focus/update handlers.

Source context: [NSCA weightlifting for sports performance position statement (2023)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36952649/). It supports appropriate programming and technique/skill development, not this individual plan or its software limits. No youth, injury rehabilitation or sport competition peak/taper generator is introduced.
