# Loadnote v3.9.0

Approachable meet prep, build 30900. Schema 32 is unchanged.

- Meet lift setup exposes start, training days, time budget and explicit competition training maxes. Evidence, detailed exposures, variations and load increments stay available under optional disclosures. Preview phase durations no longer compete with date-derived meet planning.
- Step 2 chooses the event and retains the existing reviewed timeline, quality gate and separate scheduling approval.
- Loading choices are linear, three-week waves and daily undulating. Decisions defaults to linear for beginner/unknown experience or limited lift frequency; intermediate/advanced profiles with at least two exposures per lift get a daily-undulating suggestion. This is an explainable setup rule, not a personalized outcome claim. The chosen resolved style is frozen in the reviewed source configuration.
- Daily-undulating primary/light practice uses 5/8 accumulation reps and 3/6 strength reps; light loads remain 7.5 percentage points lower. Wave behavior is unchanged and now accessible for meet base phases. Peak, taper and event-week protections remain unchanged; extended base phases still hold the last supported target rather than increasing indefinitely.
- Accessories have a curated movement picker plus custom names. Swapping resets load and equipment confirmation; bodyweight starts explicitly at zero. Priority suggestions favor saved preferred identities and do not repeat existing movements. Main/variation and avoided identities remain excluded; no loads are inferred.
- Existing linear and wave records replay unchanged. New daily-undulating records include a policy identity and are covered through save, backup normalization and approved scheduling. Input changes invalidate previews. Existing intake/symptom, time, equipment, chronology and explicit-review guards remain in effect.

Browser regressions cover essential visibility, beginner/intermediate suggestions, manual styles, day changes, accessory swaps and narrow-width layout. Physical iPhone/Safari acceptance still requires the mobile acceptance checklist; automated Chromium is not a device test.
