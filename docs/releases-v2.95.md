# Loadnote v2.95.0 — Training Goals & Sport-Aware Coaching

Athlete goals now have optional, explicitly confirmed primary and secondary priorities: powerlifting, hypertrophy, general athletic development and Olympic weightlifting. Season, sport position, recurring practice/game days and athlete-confirmed restrictions extend the existing availability, time, equipment and event fields. Legacy free-text goals retain unknown structured context; a sport name never silently becomes a prescription or priority.

Decisions, Coach and Companion share a read-only context review. Multiple matching active goals require a choice. Missing context produces a focused next question. Declared gym/sport overlaps and near-term events invite coordination, not automatic cancellation or load changes. Goal-linked cardio records describe logged minutes, not conditioning improvement or measured power. Hypertrophy uses the existing all-training four-week review and says so explicitly. Current corrected context is used, not historical as-recorded replay.

Olympic practice has a separate revisioned journal: existing exercise identity, athlete-confirmed snatch/clean/jerk/clean & jerk/pull/other family, exact variation, individual attempt load in kg, made/missed/unknown outcome and self-reported consistent/inconsistent/unknown quality. Families and variations remain distinct. Corrections and voids retain history. Unlinked records and missing exercise identities are not silently assigned to a goal. There is no technique diagnosis, judged competition result, estimated max, power measurement or inferred load increase. Do not duplicate practice in strength workouts to create PRs.

Schema 27 adds `olympicPractice`; goal context is optional. Migration preserves existing workouts, PRs, programs and goal histories. The journal participates in backups, recovery snapshots and conflict-first sync. Older schema-25/26 sync checksum bases remain valid; schema-27 packages must contain the new collection. Explicit exercise-identity merges update practice links.

Existing powerlifting prescriptions remain unchanged. Structured goals supply powerlifting target context only when powerlifting is a confirmed primary/secondary priority; legacy unknown context keeps its previous behavior. Dedicated hypertrophy, Olympic-weightlifting and sport program generators are future releases, not claims of this release. Guidance continues to reference the IUSCA hypertrophy position stand and NSCA weightlifting-for-sports-performance position statement, with athlete declarations and logging evidence clearly distinguished from general research.

Unsigned development build 29500. Unit, syntax, mobile/native source and desktop/mobile browser regression gates precede review. Native compilation, signing/distribution, physical-device acceptance and qualified-coach validation remain separate requirements.
