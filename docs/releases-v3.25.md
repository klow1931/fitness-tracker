# Loadnote v3.25.0 — A clearer first workout

## What changed

- Start with **Help me get started**, **I already have a program**, or **Just log a workout**. The guided path opens existing goal/schedule/equipment setup, then the program workspace for review. It does not silently create or schedule a plan.
- Choose **Simple view** to focus Home on the next workout and calendar. Secondary reports, training-journey cards and training-block details are hidden, not deleted. **Show all features · Full view** on Home and the Profile preference restore them. All navigation destinations remain available.
- The workout guide explains exercises, actual work, optional effort ratings, completion, rest and review/save. Next/Back are tips, never training actions. Hide it anytime; replay from **Profile → How Loadnote feels**. A guide finishes only after a new workout persists successfully, not when review opens or a save fails.
- Existing-program entry leads to templates and manual entry, with advanced adoption still reachable. A reusable template is not a completed session.

## Data and limitations

Preferences are stored only on the current device under `loadnote.experience.v1`. They are not synced or included in training backups. Clearing browser/app storage resets them. Existing users retain Full view by default; choosing Simple view never changes athlete experience, training maxes, RPE or existing plans. No schema change.

The guide is instructional, not a form-completion wizard or exercise-technique coach. Program builders retain their existing review, identity and safety prerequisites. No maxes or effort ratings are invented to bypass them.

## Acceptance checks

Exercise all three routes on a fresh profile; toggle Simple/Full and reload; verify advanced tools remain reachable. Navigate tips without touching workout fields, hide/replay, and complete a workout with unknown RPE. Force preference and workout persistence failures to verify unchanged preferences and recoverable actual work. Check 320px layout and native local-only account wording.
