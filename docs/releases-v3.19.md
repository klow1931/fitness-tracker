# Loadnote v3.19.0 — Guided training foundation

Home, Coach → Today and Decisions now expose the same read-only training hub. Getting-started steps are derived from saved setup, scheduled plans and recorded sessions, not from another onboarding database. Manual training and accounts remain optional independent paths.

Unfinished strength or sport work takes priority. Existing lifecycle policies still control overlapping programs, overdue sessions, weekly/phase boundaries and handoffs. Opening the hub does not approve or schedule anything. Profile saves refresh setup progress only after durable persistence succeeds.

The weekly card reuses v3.18 corrected-evidence reviews. Incomplete weeks show their availability date; overlapping plans require selection. Sport and hypertrophy retain their dedicated reviews. No target change is inferred from a visit, missing data or a model response.

Training tools offers a searchable exercise library with equipment, role and exact-name curated-instruction filters. Custom names and unknown equipment remain explicit; text search does not invent equipment. Existing educational cues and external sources are reused without copying videos. A library visit cannot substitute a movement or transfer its loads into a plan.

Adjust your session separates time/preferences, equipment/exercise edits and Calendar changes. These are handoffs to existing previews, fresh-target requirements and explicit approval. They do not prescribe rehabilitation or bypass draft/stale-evidence guards.

Version 3.19.0/build 31900; schema 32 unchanged. This is the first reviewable slice of the larger guided-training/native-beta milestone. Signed TestFlight/Play distribution, native service provisioning, real-device accessibility and background acceptance remain outstanding owner-controlled gates. Local model reliability on physical devices is not established by browser tests.
