# Loadnote v2.99.0

Guided starting structures connect confirmed goals and available days to the existing hypertrophy, Olympic weightlifting and athlete planners. Select exact mapped movements once; the planner creates editable slots on the selected days. Loads, increments, equipment conventions, technical variations and drill stop protocols remain explicit inputs. Every week still requires review before saving, and Calendar scheduling is separate.

Repeated hypertrophy progression now resolves the effective accepted Calendar target instead of returning to the frozen starting load. Each next selected increment requires two new fully logged sessions at that exact prescription/revision, complete effort and coverage, reported tolerance and unchanged constraints. Earlier-dose successes cannot qualify the next increase. Changed targets, deviations, incomplete work, duplicate logs, restrictions and protected lower-volume weeks hold progression.

Decisions and Coach explain the same current target and next evidence requirement. Approvals preserve completed workouts, frozen plans and the history of each previous proposal. Later-dose outcomes are attributed only to their own exact approval. Chat does not save, approve or schedule training.

Starting structures are authored by Loadnote, not attributed to a qualified human coach. They are editable planning scaffolds, not individualized doses or validated sport prescriptions. Olympic plans retain the named reported coach-review requirement. This release does not add automatic sport progression, technique diagnosis, causal learning, competition peaking or signed device acceptance. Existing schema 30 stores the approval chain; no migration is required.

Validation results and synthetic desktop/mobile screenshots are recorded in the [v2.99 walkthrough](demo-v2.99/README.md).
