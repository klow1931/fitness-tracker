# Loadnote v2.91.0 — Complete Session Programming

## Reviewed accessories, not automatic weakness diagnoses
- Both the main phase designer and quick four-week builder support optional accessory slots after primary/secondary work. A starter library suggests candidates from explicit reported priorities and matching preferred identities; athletes can enter other movements or substitutions manually.
- Each slot records stable exercise identity, day, purpose, workload group, tracking, actual equipment confirmation and an explicit starting load. Changing a name/equipment clears load and equipment confirmation. New identities enter the catalog only when the reviewed program is durably saved.
- Rep-based work supports a 6–20 rep-range envelope; within-range completed reps count as planned. Timed holds and conditioning use seconds/minutes, not invented reps, loads or training maxes.
- Pilot limits are 1–3 sets per strength slot, 3 movements/session, 24 accessory strength/hold sets/week and 12 per assigned group; RPE caps are athlete-editable within 6–8. These are software bounds, not an individualized optimal dose. Starter inputs are reviewable placeholders, not evidence-backed personal prescriptions.
- Combined time budgeting retains the existing main-lift allowance and adds 3 minutes/movement plus 3 minutes/strength set or entered conditioning duration. Over-budget plans are blocked rather than silently clipping or crowding out main lifts.
- Group counts are direct assigned-category sets, not complete muscle stimulus or physiological recovery estimates. Primary/secondary overlap requires manual review.
- Reps/load stay fixed in saved future sessions; the displayed double-progression rule requires complete effort evidence and separate review before a small increase. Nothing automatically changes Calendar targets. Holds/conditioning are manually reviewed.
- Deload reduces accessory sets/conditioning duration and caps effort at 6. Meet-cycle accumulation/strength retain accessories, including extended phases; peak, taper and event weeks omit them explicitly.

## Integrity and existing Decisions
Accessories survive preview, save, import/backup normalization, scheduling and workout loading. Saved configurations regenerate exactly; drift fails validation. Whole-cycle quality checks verify accessory targets against their own reviewed configuration, not competition training maxes. Existing lift reviews leave accessory targets untouched and omit them from lift/max adjustment maps. Historical programs without accessories retain their original configuration and sessions; existing workouts are not rewritten. Schema remains 25.

## Validation and boundaries
Domain coverage includes identity/aliases, avoidance, time/set ceilings, reps/holds/cardio, in-range adherence, immutable review, Calendar, meet-cycle save/schedule, peak/taper exclusion, drift rejection and independent lift-review safety. Browser smoke scenarios cover both editors, explicit-load/confirmation guards, substitution invalidation, saving, scheduling, logger loading and priority suggestions.

Unsigned native build 29100. No signing, Apple enrollment, TestFlight or store submission. Physical-device acceptance remains required. Conversational/online Coach changes are reserved for v2.92; the native beta remains local-only.

General training background: [ACSM progression position stand (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19204579/) discusses exercise ordering, rep ranges and performance-contingent progression. It does not validate this accessory policy, its pilot ceilings, time estimates, or a specific athlete's training dose; the app's defaults are deliberately separately identified as engineering choices.
