# Loadnote v4.9 milestone — Relevant offline references

Delivered with the other Coach milestones in **v5.0.0**.

Rank educational questions against curated coaching summaries and explicitly saved Europe PMC excerpts. Bounded token expansion covers common synonyms and a few known spellings. Title/tag matches and document frequency influence ranking; relevance is lexical and does not measure study quality, certainty or general intelligence. Unsupported questions can return no match.

The reference explorer exposes curated source links, saved-paper provenance, retrieval dates and limitations. Relevant saved literature is included alongside curated summaries instead of being crowded out by duplicate concept matches. Source questions in Coach can use that same local material. Personal log questions, medical requests, existing RPE definitions and reviewed training actions keep their authoritative handlers.

Saved source links are rebuilt from validated PubMed/PMC IDs. Future retrieved/publication records are excluded. Short follow-ups resolve from the athlete’s educational question, not assistant assertions. Research sources remain separate from athlete evidence and never authorize an adjustment. Reference ranking makes no network request and does not retrain weights. Optional internet research keeps the existing v4.7 permission controls; returned publications also consider topical overlap and date when ordered.
