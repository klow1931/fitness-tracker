# Loadnote v2.96.0 — Reviewed Hypertrophy Programming

Dedicated resistance-training planning now complements the strength/meet builders and sport-aware coaching. Choose an active confirmed hypertrophy goal, catalog exercises with confirmed muscle assignments, actual equipment, explicit starting loads and their conventions, rep ranges, sets, effort caps, rest and available increments. No load is derived from a strength estimate or competition-lift training max.

Preview all weeks, time allowances, separate direct/indirect workload, reviewed target discrepancies and four-week history coverage. Dose limits are software bounds, not individualized recommendations. Optional lower-volume weeks reduce sets and effort caps without silently changing loads. Reduce/defer work manually if the cap cannot be respected.

Saving requires separate plan and workload approvals; it does not schedule, replace or activate training. Scheduling is a separate confirmation, preserving open drafts and refusing date/workout conflicts, overlapping reviewed cycles, stale context, restrictions and nearby events. Frozen review snapshots persist with backup/export/import and sync (schema 28; old manifests remain verifiable).

Calendar, current-program viewing and logging retain rep ranges, explicit load conventions and rest allowances. Set guidance accepts repetitions inside the reviewed range without automatically raising the next load. Coach and Decisions share the same read-only progression report: two comparable fully logged normal sessions at the top of the range, complete athlete-declared history and tolerated context can prompt review of the athlete-selected increment. Missing effort, ambiguous/late plans, unresolved work, changed context or protected lower-volume intent block that prompt. Future targets never change automatically. Hypertrophy completion does not enter the squat/bench/deadlift transition handoff.

Evidence context: [IUSCA hypertrophy position stand (2021)](https://doi.org/10.47206/ijsc.v1i1.81). It does not individually validate generated proposals. Muscle assignments and tolerance remain athlete declarations, not measured stimulus/recovery. No Olympic or sport-specific program generator, injury rehabilitation, technique diagnosis or automatic progression is introduced.
