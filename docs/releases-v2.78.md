# Loadnote v2.78.0 — Progress Stories

- Reworked the default Progress experience around four evidence-backed stories: Strength, Consistency, Milestones, and Recent changes.
- Reuses the existing demonstrated-capacity, schedule/adherence, and accepted-review history rather than creating a new score or analytics engine.
- Strength stories preserve sparse-evidence guards and never substitute planned loads, training maxes, or known 1RMs for logged performance evidence.
- Added conservative milestone summaries such as a new high within the selected story window; these are explicitly not presented as tested all-time PRs.
- Consistency keeps completed, skipped, unresolved, and cancelled sessions separate and only reports resolved-session adherence when scheduled evidence exists.
- Recent changes surface accepted programming decisions with their stored reason/evidence without claiming the change caused a later result.
- Moved detailed movement evidence, adherence breakdowns, programming history, Training Review, record-book tools, measurements, and photos behind Explore Progress while preserving their existing functionality.
- Schema remains v25. Workout history, internal kg storage, kg/lb conversion, RPE/e1RM math, training-max logic, progression, fatigue/status, adaptive programming, and meet-cycle policy are unchanged.
