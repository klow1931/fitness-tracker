Privacy policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026 · Simple summary for Loadnote (web).

What Loadnote is

Loadnote is a local-first training log that runs in your browser. You do not need an account to use the core features.

Data we store on your device

Workout history, nutrition logs, body measurements, progress photos you add, goals, programs, templates, form self-check notes, and settings are stored in this browser on this device (IndexedDB / localStorage). Core training data is stored locally in this web build. Optional AI requests may send relevant context to the configured AI service through the Loadnote backend when enabled.

What leaves your device

  • If you paste your own API key and send a chat message, the prompt and related context are sent to the AI provider you chose (for example xAI or OpenAI). Keys stay in this browser and are not included in JSON backups.
  • Barcode food lookup: If you look up a barcode online, the barcode number is requested from Open Food Facts. Their privacy terms apply to that request.
  • Libraries / CDN: The app may load scripts or styles from third-party CDNs (for example Chart.js, Tailwind, barcode scanner). Those providers may see standard network request data (IP, browser type) like any website.
  • Hosting: If you open Loadnote on GitHub Pages or another host, that host delivers the app files. Your training log still stays in your browser unless you export it yourself.

What we do not do (this web build)

  • No Loadnote user accounts or login
  • No sale of your workout or nutrition data
  • No advertising SDK in this build
  • Progress photos stay on-device unless you export them

Backups & deletion

Use Tools → Export JSON backup (or CSV) to copy your data. Clearing site data for this site in your browser deletes local Loadnote data on that device. Uninstalling or switching browsers/profiles does not move data automatically.

Children

Loadnote is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it to store data about children if applicable laws require parental consent you cannot provide.

Health disclaimer

Loadnote and the AI Coach are not medical care. Information is for general fitness tracking only — not diagnosis, treatment, or personalized medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes in a future version, the date at the top will be updated. Continued use after an update means you see the new text in the app.

Contact

For questions about this app, contact the person who shared Loadnote with you (the project maintainer).
