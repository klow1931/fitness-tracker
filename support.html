Support

Contact the Loadnote maintainer through the project issue tracker. For a reproducible app problem, include app version, device/OS, steps and the displayed error.

Issues are public. Do not attach personal backups, account credentials, health information, workout history or photos. Request a private contact channel before sending sensitive information. A verified confidential privacy/support email must be supplied by the owner before a public store release.

Before reinstalling, save a JSON backup outside the app and check that it exists. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove your only copy.
