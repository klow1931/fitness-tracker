Privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026. Loadnote is maintained through the Loadnote project. Contact and support.

Local training data

The packaged Android beta is local-only. Workouts, goals, effort reports, nutrition, measurements, bodyweight and selected photos are stored on your device for logging, charts and explainable training guidance. No advertising or analytics SDK is bundled. Account, cloud training sync, paid online Coach and realtime voice are disabled in this native build.

Optional network features

Barcode lookup sends the requested barcode to Open Food Facts and exposes normal connection information such as your IP address. Camera access for browser scanning requires your permission; barcode lookup does not send workout history. Optional local AI downloads a runtime and model from third-party hosts after confirmation; training inference then runs on the device. Those hosts receive normal download connection information.

On separately configured web deployments, sign-in uses the operator’s identity provider. Explicit remote sync stores your structured training package on that operator’s server. Online Coach sends the selected training context and question to the configured AI provider. Realtime voice sends audio and selected workout context to its provider after consent. The web operator must publish its identity, confidential contact, provider names, processing terms and retention schedule before enabling those services for public users. This native policy does not establish those operator terms.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or erase app/browser data. Use the record history views to delete individual entries and Tools to export backups. Uninstalling the native app or clearing its storage removes its local database; exported files and externally shared copies remain in the destinations you selected. Recovery snapshots are local copies, not external backups. JSON backups exclude photo image data and unfinished workout drafts; export photos separately if needed.

For a configured web account, Profile → Account → Delete account removes the account and its remote training package from the active server store. It does not erase device data, external exports, identity-provider accounts or provider-held data. Separate server backups and provider retention require the operator’s published policy. Deletion instructions.

Your choices

Use the app without an account in the local-only beta. Export your records, review before importing a replacement, revoke camera permission, disable local AI and clear app or browser storage to delete local data. Training guidance is not medical diagnosis or clinical clearance. Do not share sensitive records in public support issues.
