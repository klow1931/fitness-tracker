Delete data or account

Packaged Android beta

This build does not create a Loadnote cloud account. Delete individual records in their history views. To remove all local records, use Android Settings → Apps → Loadnote → Storage → Clear storage. Export any records you want to keep first. Delete separately exported backups and photos from their saved destinations.

Configured web accounts

  1. Open your operator’s Loadnote app and sign in.
  2. Open Profile → Account and choose Delete account.
  3. Read and confirm deletion. The server removes the account and active remote training package and clears the session.
  4. Clear app or browser storage to erase device data separately, and remove external exports if required.

If you cannot sign in, contact the operator through its published private support address. Do not post identity documents or training data in the public issue tracker. Loadnote cannot delete your identity-provider account or independently held AI-provider records; request those deletions from the relevant provider.
